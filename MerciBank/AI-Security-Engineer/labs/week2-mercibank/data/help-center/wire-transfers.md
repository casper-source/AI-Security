# Sending a wire transfer (MerciBank help centre)

MerciBank customers can send domestic wires from the app or by asking Merci.

1. Tell Merci the amount and the recipient's account.
2. Merci sends a one-time passcode (OTP) by SMS to the number on file.
3. Enter the OTP. The transfer is only submitted after a valid OTP.

Daily limits apply per account. Wires above your daily limit need to be split across days or raised
by our team after identity verification. OTP is always required — there is no way to skip it.
